# Privacy Policy and Support Information

**Last updated:** September 30, 2026

### 1. Overview
Sag-City ("we", "our", or "us") provides virtual private network (VPN) services designed to protect your privacy and security. This document outlines our data handling practices and support channels.

### 2. Information We Do Not Collect (No-Logs Policy)
We strictly respect your privacy:
- We do not monitor, record, log, or store your browsing activity, websites visited, DNS queries, or transmitted data.
- We do not log your real IP address or the destination IP addresses you connect to.

### 3. Information We May Collect
- Minimal Diagnostic/Operational Data: We may collect anonymized crash reports or aggregate bandwidth metrics solely to maintain service quality and server performance.
- Account / Contact Information: If you contact our support team, we retain your email address and message history solely to resolve your inquiry.

### 4. Security
We use industry-standard encryption protocols to safeguard your traffic while connected to our VPN network.

### 5. Third-Party Services
If our app integrates third-party services, their collection of information is governed by their respective privacy policies.
